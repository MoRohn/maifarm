# 💻 Science & Tech Jokes Collection

For the nerds, geeks, and everyone who appreciates intelligent humor!

## Programming Jokes

1. **Why do programmers prefer dark mode?**  
   Because light attracts bugs!

2. **How many programmers does it take to change a light bulb?**  
   None. That's a hardware problem.

3. **Why do Java developers wear glasses?**  
   Because they don't C#!

4. **A SQL query walks into a bar, walks up to two tables and asks:**  
   "Can I join you?"

5. **Why did the developer go broke?**  
   Because he used up all his cache!

6. **"Knock, knock."**  
   "Who's there?"  
   [very long pause]  
   "Java."

7. **There are only 10 types of people in the world:**  
   Those who understand binary and those who don't.

8. **Why did the programmer quit his job?**  
   Because he didn't get arrays!

9. **What's the object-oriented way to become wealthy?**  
   Inheritance.

10. **Why did the functions stop calling each other?**  
    Because they had constant arguments.

## Science Jokes

21. **A photon checks into a hotel.**  
    Bellhop: "Can I help you with your luggage?"  
    Photon: "No thanks, I'm traveling light."

22. **Why can't you trust an atom?**  
    Because they make up everything!

23. **Helium walks into a bar.**  
    The bartender says, "We don't serve noble gases here."  
    Helium doesn't react.

24. **Two atoms are walking down the street.**  
    One says, "I think I lost an electron!"  
    The other asks, "Are you positive?"

25. **Why did the physicist break up with the biologist?**  
    There was no chemistry.

26. **What do you call an acid with an attitude?**  
    A-mean-oh acid!

27. **Why did the chicken cross the Möbius strip?**  
    To get to the same side!

28. **Schrödinger's cat walks into a bar.**  
    And doesn't.

29. **A neutron walks into a bar and asks, "How much for a beer?"**  
    The bartender says, "For you, no charge."

30. **Einstein, Newton, and Pascal are playing hide and seek.**  
    Einstein counts while Newton and Pascal hide. Pascal runs off and hides. Newton draws a square on the ground and stands in it. Einstein finishes counting, opens his eyes, and says, "Newton, I found you!"  
    Newton says, "No, you found Pascal! I'm Newton per square meter!"
