# 💬 One-Liner Collection

Quick wit in a single sentence - perfect for any occasion!

## Observational One-Liners

1. **I haven't spoken to my wife in years.** I didn't want to interrupt her.

2. **My therapist says I have a preoccupation with vengeance.** We'll see about that.

3. **I told my wife she was drawing her eyebrows too high.** She looked surprised.

4. **My wife told me to stop acting like a flamingo,** so I had to put my foot down.

5. **I went to buy some camouflage trousers yesterday,** but couldn't find any.

6. **I haven't slept for ten days,** because that would be too long.

7. **Someone stole my Microsoft Office and they're gonna pay.** You have my Word.

8. **I hate Russian dolls,** they're so full of themselves.

9. **A clear conscience is usually the sign of a bad memory.**

10. **I didn't think orthopedic shoes would help,** but I stand corrected.

## Self-Deprecating Humor

11. **I'm great at multitasking.** I can waste time, be unproductive, and procrastinate all at once.

12. **I'm not saying I'm Batman.** I'm just saying no one has ever seen me and Batman in a room together.

13. **My bed is a magical place where I suddenly remember everything I forgot to do.**

14. **I'm not lazy,** I'm just on energy-saving mode.

15. **I put the "pro" in procrastinate.**

16. **I don't have a beer gut,** I have a protective covering for my rock hard abs.

17. **I'm not arguing,** I'm just explaining why I'm right.

18. **I'm not short,** I'm fun-sized.

19. **My wallet is like an onion,** opening it makes me cry.

20. **I'm not clumsy.** The floor just hates me, the table and chairs are bullies, and the walls get in my way.

## Witty Observations

21. **Light travels faster than sound,** which is why some people appear bright until they speak.

22. **The problem with kleptomaniacs is that they always take things literally.**

23. **I used to think I was indecisive,** but now I'm not too sure.

24. **Nostalgia isn't what it used to be.**

25. **The early bird might get the worm,** but the second mouse gets the cheese.

26. **Change is inevitable,** except from a vending machine.

27. **If at first you don't succeed,** skydiving is not for you.

28. **Money talks,** but all mine ever says is goodbye.

29. **Knowledge is knowing a tomato is a fruit;** wisdom is not putting it in a fruit salad.

30. **The problem isn't that obesity runs in your family.** It's that no one runs in your family.

## Relationship One-Liners

31. **My wife and I were happy for twenty years;** then we met.

32. **Marriage is like a deck of cards.** In the beginning all you need is two hearts and a diamond. By the end, you wish you had a club and a spade.

33. **Love is blind;** marriage is the eye-opener.

34. **My girlfriend told me to go out and get something that makes her look sexy,** so I got drunk.

35. **Behind every great man is a woman rolling her eyes.**

36. **Women call me ugly until they find out how much money I make.** Then they call me ugly and poor.

37. **My wife gave me an ultimatum: her or my addiction to sweets.** It was a tough decision, but I'm sure she'll do fine on her own.

38. **Marriage is a relationship in which one person is always right,** and the other is the husband.

39. **I love being married.** It's so great to find one special person you want to annoy for the rest of your life.

40. **My wife told me I had to stop acting like a flamingo.** So I had to put my foot down.

## Tech & Modern Life

41. **My internet is so slow,** it's just faster to drive to the Google headquarters and ask them in person.

42. **I changed my password to "incorrect"** so whenever I forget it, the computer will say "Your password is incorrect."

43. **My email password has been hacked.** That's the third time I've had to rename the cat.

44. **I hate when I'm singing a song and the artist gets the words wrong.**

45. **The WiFi went down for five minutes,** so I had to talk to my family. They seem like nice people.

46. **I'm not addicted to my phone.** We're just in a very committed relationship.

47. **Autocorrect makes me say things I didn't Nintendo.**

48. **I put my phone in airplane mode,** but it's not flying.

49. **My computer beat me at chess,** but it was no match for me at kickboxing.

50. **The internet: where men are men, women are men,** and children are FBI agents.

## Philosophical One-Liners

51. **I think, therefore I am single.**

52. **If you think nobody cares if you're alive,** try missing a couple of car payments.

53. **The last thing I want to do is hurt you,** but it's still on my list.

54. **We live in a society where pizza gets to your house before the police.**

55. **If I agreed with you,** we'd both be wrong.

56. **War does not determine who is right,** only who is left.

57. **Politicians and diapers have one thing in common:** they should both be changed regularly, and for the same reason.

58. **A diplomat is someone who tells you to go to hell** in such a way that you look forward to the trip.

59. **I don't suffer from insanity;** I enjoy every minute of it.

60. **They say money doesn't bring happiness,** but everyone still wants to prove it for themselves.
