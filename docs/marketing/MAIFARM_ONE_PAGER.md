# MaiFarm - Multi-Agent AI Orchestration Platform

## Revolutionize Your Development Workflow with Collaborative AI

### The Problem
Modern software development teams struggle with increasing complexity, resource constraints, and delivery pressure. Single-developer productivity hasn't scaled with project demands.

### The Solution
MaiFarm orchestrates multiple AI agents to work collaboratively on software projects—like having an entire development team that works 24/7, never makes mistakes, and scales instantly.

---

## How It Works

**1. Deploy a "Farm"** → Launch multiple specialized AI agents
**2. Agents Collaborate** → Work together in real-time on your project
**3. Harvest Results** → Automatically collect and integrate completed work
