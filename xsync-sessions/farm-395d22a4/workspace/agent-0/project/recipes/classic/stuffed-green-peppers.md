# Classic Stuffed Green Peppers

## Description
Traditional comfort food featuring tender green bell peppers filled with a savory mixture of seasoned ground beef, rice, and melted cheese. This family-favorite recipe has been passed down through generations.

## Ingredients

### For the Peppers:
- 6 large green bell peppers, tops cut and seeds removed
- 1 tablespoon olive oil
- Salt for sprinkling

### For the Filling:
- 1 lb ground beef (85/15 lean)
- 1 medium onion, finely diced
- 3 cloves garlic, minced
- 1 cup cooked white rice
- 1 can (14.5 oz) diced tomatoes, drained
- 1 can (8 oz) tomato sauce
- 1 teaspoon Italian seasoning
- 1 teaspoon salt
- 1/2 teaspoon black pepper
- 1/2 teaspoon paprika
- 1 1/2 cups shredded mozzarella cheese (divided)
- 2 tablespoons fresh parsley, chopped

### For the Baking Dish:
- 1 cup beef broth or water
- 1/2 cup tomato sauce

## Instructions

### Preparation (20 minutes):
1. **Prepare the peppers:** Cut tops off peppers and remove seeds and membranes. If peppers won't stand upright, carefully trim a small slice from the bottom without creating a hole.

2. **Par-cook the peppers:** Place peppers in a large pot of boiling salted water for 3-5 minutes until slightly softened. Remove and drain upside down on paper towels.

### Cooking (45 minutes):
1. **Make the filling:** 
   - Heat olive oil in a large skillet over medium-high heat
   - Add ground beef, breaking it up with a spoon
   - Cook until browned, about 5-7 minutes
   - Add onion and garlic, cook until softened (3-4 minutes)
   
2. **Season the mixture:**
   - Stir in cooked rice, diced tomatoes, 1/2 cup tomato sauce
   - Add Italian seasoning, salt, pepper, and paprika
   - Mix in 1 cup of mozzarella cheese and parsley
   - Cook for 2-3 minutes until heated through

3. **Stuff the peppers:**
   - Preheat oven to 350°F (175°C)
   - Stand peppers upright in a baking dish
   - Fill each pepper with the beef and rice mixture
   - Mix beef broth with remaining tomato sauce and pour around peppers

4. **Bake:**
   - Cover dish with foil and bake for 35-40 minutes
   - Remove foil, top each pepper with remaining cheese
   - Bake uncovered for 10 more minutes until cheese melts

## Serving Suggestions
- Serve hot with crusty bread to soak up the sauce
- Pair with a simple green salad
- Garnish with fresh parsley or basil

## Variations
- **Turkey version:** Substitute ground turkey for beef
- **Mexican style:** Use Mexican cheese blend, add cumin and chili powder
- **Italian sausage:** Replace half the beef with Italian sausage
- **Vegetarian:** Use plant-based ground meat substitute
- **Low-carb:** Replace rice with cauliflower rice

## Storage Tips
- **Refrigerate:** Store covered for up to 4 days
- **Freeze:** Wrap individually in foil, freeze up to 3 months
- **Reheat:** Microwave 2-3 minutes or oven at 350°F for 15-20 minutes

## Chef's Tips
- Choose peppers that are similar in size for even cooking
- Don't overfill peppers - leave 1/2 inch space at top
- For firmer peppers, skip the par-boiling step
- Add a pinch of red pepper flakes for heat
- Use day-old rice for better texture
