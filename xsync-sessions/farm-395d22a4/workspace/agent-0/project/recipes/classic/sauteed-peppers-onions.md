# Classic Sautéed Peppers and Onions

## Description
A versatile and simple dish that's a staple in many cuisines. Perfect as a side dish, sandwich topping, or base for countless recipes. The sweet caramelized onions complement the tender-crisp peppers beautifully.

## Ingredients

- 3 large green bell peppers, sliced into strips
- 1 red bell pepper, sliced (for color)
- 2 large onions, sliced into half-moons
- 3 tablespoons olive oil
- 3 cloves garlic, minced
- 1 teaspoon salt
- 1/2 teaspoon black pepper
- 1/2 teaspoon dried oregano
- 1/4 teaspoon red pepper flakes (optional)
- 2 tablespoons balsamic vinegar (optional)
- Fresh basil for garnish (optional)

## Instructions

### Preparation (10 minutes):
1. Slice all peppers into 1/2-inch strips
2. Cut onions into similar-sized half-moons
3. Mince garlic
4. Have all seasonings ready

### Cooking (20 minutes):

1. **Heat the pan:**
   - Use large skillet or cast iron pan
   - Heat olive oil over medium-high heat
   - Oil should shimmer but not smoke

2. **Cook the onions (5 minutes):**
   - Add onions to hot oil
   - Stir to coat with oil
   - Cook until starting to soften
   - Season with pinch of salt

3. **Add the peppers (12-15 minutes):**
   - Add all pepper strips
   - Toss to combine with onions
   - Season with salt and pepper
   - Cook, stirring occasionally
   - Peppers should be tender but not mushy

4. **Finish with flavor (2 minutes):**
   - Add garlic, oregano, red pepper flakes
   - Cook until garlic is fragrant
   - Optional: Add balsamic vinegar
   - Toss everything together
   - Taste and adjust seasoning

## Recipe Variations

### Italian Style
- Add fresh tomatoes
- Include fresh basil and oregano
- Finish with Parmesan cheese

### Mexican Style
- Add cumin and chili powder
- Include jalapeños
- Squeeze lime juice at end

### Mediterranean Style
- Add kalamata olives
- Include capers
- Top with feta cheese

### Asian Fusion
- Use sesame oil
- Add soy sauce and ginger
- Garnish with sesame seeds

## Make-Ahead Tips
- Prep vegetables night before
- Store sliced peppers and onions separately
- Can be made 3 days ahead and reheated
- Freezes well for up to 3 months

## Reheating Methods
- **Stovetop:** Medium heat with splash of water
- **Microwave:** 1-2 minutes, stirring halfway
- **Oven:** 350°F for 10 minutes
- Add fresh herbs after reheating

## Chef's Pro Tips
- Don't overcrowd the pan
- Keep heat medium-high for caramelization
- Add splash of water if sticking
- Salt draws out moisture - add gradually
- Leave some char for smoky flavor
