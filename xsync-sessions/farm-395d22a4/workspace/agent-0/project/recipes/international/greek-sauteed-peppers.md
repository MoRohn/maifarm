# Greek Sautéed Green Peppers (Prasines Piperies Tiganistes)

## Description
A simple yet flavorful Greek side dish featuring tender green bell peppers sautéed with olive oil, red onions, and aromatic herbs. This traditional Mediterranean recipe brings out the natural sweetness of the peppers.

## Ingredients

- 6 medium green bell peppers, cut into strips
- 1/3 cup extra virgin olive oil
- 1 large red onion, sliced into half-moons
- 3 cloves garlic, minced
- 2 tablespoons fresh parsley, chopped
- 1 tablespoon fresh oregano (or 1 tsp dried)
- 1/2 cup crushed tomatoes (optional)
- 2 tablespoons red wine vinegar
- Salt and black pepper to taste
- 1/2 teaspoon sugar
- Crumbled feta cheese for serving (optional)

## Instructions

### Preparation (10 minutes):
1. Wash peppers and cut into 1-inch wide strips
2. Remove seeds and white membranes
3. Slice onion into thin half-moons
4. Mince garlic and chop fresh herbs

### Cooking (25 minutes):
1. **Heat the oil:** In a large skillet, heat olive oil over medium heat

2. **Sauté aromatics:**
   - Add red onion and cook until softened (5 minutes)
   - Add garlic and cook for 1 minute until fragrant

3. **Cook the peppers:**
   - Add pepper strips to the pan
   - Season with salt, pepper, and sugar
   - Stir to coat with oil
   - Cook for 15-20 minutes, stirring occasionally

4. **Add finishing touches:**
   - If using tomatoes, add them halfway through cooking
   - Add oregano and half the parsley
   - Drizzle with red wine vinegar
   - Cook until peppers are tender but not mushy

5. **Final seasoning:**
   - Taste and adjust salt and pepper
   - Garnish with remaining parsley

## Serving Suggestions
- Serve warm or at room temperature
- Excellent with grilled meats or fish
- Serve with crusty bread to soak up the oil
- Top with crumbled feta cheese
- Part of a mezze platter

## Traditional Greek Variations
- **Spetsai style:** Add capers and olives
- **Island version:** Include cherry tomatoes
- **Winter style:** Add preserved lemon
- **Festive version:** Include pine nuts

## Storage Tips
- Room temperature: Up to 4 hours
- Refrigerate: 5 days in airtight container
- Serve cold as a salad or reheat gently
- Flavors improve overnight

## Chef's Tips
- Use high-quality Greek olive oil
- Don't rush - slow cooking develops flavor
- Peppers should be silky, not crispy
- Add a splash of ouzo for authentic flavor
- Can be made ahead for parties

## Nutritional Benefits
- High in vitamin C
- Rich in antioxidants
- Heart-healthy olive oil
- Low calorie side dish
