# Quick Pepper Steak Stir-Fry

## Description
A fast and flavorful Asian-inspired dish featuring tender beef strips and crisp green peppers in a savory sauce. Ready in under 30 minutes, perfect for busy weeknight dinners.

## Ingredients

### For the Stir-Fry:
- 1 lb beef sirloin or flank steak, sliced thin against grain
- 2 large green bell peppers, cut into strips
- 1 red bell pepper, cut into strips (for color)
- 1 medium onion, sliced
- 3 cloves garlic, minced
- 1 tablespoon fresh ginger, minced
- 2 tablespoons vegetable oil (divided)
- 2 green onions, sliced for garnish
- Sesame seeds for garnish

### For the Marinade:
- 2 tablespoons soy sauce
- 1 tablespoon cornstarch
- 1 teaspoon sesame oil

### For the Sauce:
- 3 tablespoons soy sauce
- 2 tablespoons oyster sauce
- 1 tablespoon rice wine or dry sherry
- 1 teaspoon sugar
- 1 teaspoon cornstarch
- 1/4 cup beef broth
- 1/2 teaspoon black pepper
- 1/4 teaspoon red pepper flakes (optional)

## Instructions

### Preparation (10 minutes):
1. **Marinate the beef:**
   - Combine beef with marinade ingredients
   - Mix well and let sit while preparing other ingredients

2. **Prepare vegetables:**
   - Cut all peppers into uniform strips
   - Slice onion, mince garlic and ginger

3. **Mix sauce:**
   - Whisk all sauce ingredients in a bowl
   - Set aside

### Cooking (15 minutes):
1. **Stir-fry the beef (3-4 minutes):**
   - Heat wok or large skillet over high heat
   - Add 1 tablespoon oil
   - Stir-fry beef in batches until browned
   - Remove and set aside

2. **Cook vegetables (5-6 minutes):**
   - Add remaining oil to wok
   - Stir-fry onions for 1 minute
   - Add peppers, cook 3-4 minutes until crisp-tender
   - Add garlic and ginger, cook 30 seconds

3. **Combine and finish (2 minutes):**
   - Return beef to wok
   - Pour sauce over everything
   - Toss until sauce thickens and coats
   - Remove from heat immediately

## Serving Suggestions
- Serve over steamed white or brown rice
- Try with rice noodles or lo mein
- Garnish with green onions and sesame seeds
- Add a side of steamed broccoli

## Time-Saving Tips
- Buy pre-sliced stir-fry beef
- Prep all ingredients before starting
- Use frozen pepper strips in a pinch
- Double the sauce recipe for future use
- Cook rice in rice cooker while stir-frying

## Variations
- **Spicy version:** Add fresh chili peppers
- **Vegetarian:** Use firm tofu or tempeh
- **Low-carb:** Serve over cauliflower rice
- **Extra vegetables:** Add snap peas, mushrooms, or baby corn

## Chef's Quick Tips
- Freeze beef 20 minutes for easier slicing
- Keep heat high for proper "wok hei" flavor
- Don't overcrowd the pan
- Peppers should stay slightly crispy
- Have everything ready before you start cooking

## Make-Ahead Options
- Slice beef and vegetables night before
- Mix sauce in advance
- Marinate beef up to 24 hours
- Leftovers reheat well for lunch
