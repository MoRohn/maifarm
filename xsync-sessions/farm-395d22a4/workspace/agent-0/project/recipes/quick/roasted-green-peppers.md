# Simple Roasted Green Peppers

## Description
The easiest way to bring out the natural sweetness of green peppers. Roasting creates a delicious charred exterior while keeping the inside tender. Perfect as a side dish or ingredient for other recipes.

## Ingredients

- 4-6 green bell peppers, whole or halved
- 3 tablespoons olive oil
- 1 teaspoon kosher salt
- 1/2 teaspoon black pepper
- 2 cloves garlic, minced (optional)
- 1 teaspoon dried herbs (oregano, thyme, or Italian seasoning)
- Balsamic vinegar for drizzling (optional)

## Instructions

### Method 1: Whole Roasted (20 minutes)
1. **Prepare:**
   - Preheat oven to 450°F (230°C)
   - Wash and dry peppers
   - Leave whole, poke 2-3 holes with knife

2. **Season:**
   - Rub with olive oil
   - Sprinkle with salt and pepper

3. **Roast:**
   - Place on baking sheet
   - Roast 15-20 minutes
   - Turn halfway through
   - Skin should be charred and blistered

### Method 2: Halved and Roasted (15 minutes)
1. **Prepare:**
   - Cut peppers in half lengthwise
   - Remove seeds and membranes
   - Brush with olive oil mixture

2. **Roast:**
   - Place cut-side down on baking sheet
   - Roast 12-15 minutes until charred
   - Flip and roast 3 more minutes

### Method 3: Broiler Method (10 minutes)
1. **Setup:**
   - Position rack 6 inches from broiler
   - Preheat broiler on high

2. **Broil:**
   - Place peppers on foil-lined pan
   - Broil 4-5 minutes per side
   - Watch carefully to prevent burning

## Quick Serving Ideas
- Slice and add to sandwiches
- Chop for pasta salads
- Top pizzas or flatbreads
- Mix into scrambled eggs
- Blend into hummus

## 5-Minute Flavor Boosters
After roasting, try these quick additions:
- Drizzle with balsamic glaze
- Sprinkle with feta cheese
- Toss with lemon juice and herbs
- Add capers and olives
- Top with toasted pine nuts

## Storage & Meal Prep
- **Refrigerate:** Up to 5 days in airtight container
- **Freeze:** Up to 3 months in freezer bags
- **Oil storage:** Cover with olive oil in jar, keeps 2 weeks
- **Batch roasting:** Roast weekly for quick meals

## Uses for Roasted Peppers
- Antipasto platters
- Grain bowls
- Wraps and sandwiches
- Pasta dishes
- Soups and stews
- Omelets and frittatas

## Chef's Speed Tips
- Line pan with parchment for easy cleanup
- Roast alongside other vegetables
- Use convection setting to reduce time by 25%
- No need to peel unless recipe requires
- Season while still hot for better absorption
