# Complete Guide to Green Pepper Selection, Storage & Preparation

## How to Select Perfect Green Peppers

### Visual Inspection
- **Color:** Deep, uniform green color
- **Skin:** Smooth, glossy, and firm
- **Shape:** Well-formed without deformities
- **Weight:** Should feel heavy for their size

### What to Avoid
- Wrinkled or shriveled skin
- Soft spots or bruises
- Moldy or darkened stems
- Dull, pale color
- Lightweight peppers (indicates dryness)

### Ripeness Facts
- Green peppers are unripe versions of red, yellow, or orange peppers
- Slightly bitter taste is normal
- Will not ripen further after picking
- Firmer texture than ripe peppers

## Storage Methods

### Counter Storage (2-3 days)
- Room temperature for immediate use
- Keep away from direct sunlight
- Store in cool, dry place
- Best for peppers you'll use quickly

### Refrigerator Storage (1-2 weeks)

#### Whole Peppers
1. Do NOT wash before storing
2. Place in vegetable crisper drawer
3. Store in perforated plastic bag
4. Keep humidity moderate
5. Check regularly for soft spots

#### Cut Peppers
1. Remove seeds and membranes
2. Wrap tightly in plastic wrap
3. Place in airtight container
4. Use within 3-4 days
5. Pat dry before using

### Freezer Storage (6-12 months)

#### Method 1: Flash Freezing
1. Wash and dry thoroughly
2. Cut into desired shapes (strips, rings, diced)
3. Spread on parchment-lined baking sheet
4. Freeze for 2 hours until solid
5. Transfer to freezer bags
6. Remove air and label with date

#### Method 2: Blanching Method
1. Boil water in large pot
2. Blanch pepper pieces for 2-3 minutes
3. Transfer to ice bath immediately
4. Drain and pat completely dry
5. Package in freezer bags

#### Freezing Tips
- No need to thaw before cooking
- Best for cooked dishes, not raw eating
- Texture will be softer after thawing
- Freeze in meal-sized portions
- Label with date and contents

## Preparation Techniques

### Basic Prep
1. **Washing:** Rinse under cold water, pat dry
2. **Cutting:** Use sharp knife for clean cuts
3. **Deseeding:** Cut around stem, pull out core
4. **Membrane removal:** Scrape with spoon

### Cutting Styles

#### Strips (Julienne)
- Best for: Stir-fries, fajitas
- Cut pepper in half lengthwise
- Remove seeds and membranes
- Slice into 1/4-inch strips

#### Diced
- Best for: Salsas, omelets
- Cut into strips first
- Turn and cut perpendicular
- Aim for uniform size

#### Rings
- Best for: Garnishes, salads
- Slice horizontally
- Remove seeds from each ring
- Keep thickness consistent

### Special Preparations

#### For Stuffing
1. Cut top 1/2 inch from pepper
2. Remove seeds and membranes carefully
3. Trim small slice from bottom if needed for stability
4. Par-boil for 3-5 minutes if desired

#### For Roasting
1. Leave whole or halve
2. Brush with oil
3. Pierce whole peppers to prevent bursting
4. Char over flame or under broiler

## Professional Storage Hacks

### Extending Freshness
- Wrap individually in paper towels before bagging
- Store away from ethylene-producing fruits
- Keep stems attached until use
- Don't store near tomatoes or apples

### Reviving Wilted Peppers
1. Trim 1/4 inch from bottom
2. Stand in cup of cold water for 30 minutes
3. Pat dry before using
4. Best for cooking, not raw consumption

### Batch Prep Solutions
- **Sunday prep:** Wash, cut, and store for week
- **Portion control:** Divide into recipe-sized amounts
- **Mix varieties:** Combine colors for convenience
- **Label everything:** Include prep date and intended use

## Preservation Methods

### Pickling
- Quick pickle in vinegar solution
- Keeps 2-3 months refrigerated
- Great for sandwiches and salads

### Dehydrating
- Slice thin and dehydrate at 125°F
- Store in airtight containers
- Rehydrate in warm water for cooking

### Oil Preservation
- Roast peppers first
- Pack in sterile jars
- Cover with olive oil
- Refrigerate and use within 2 weeks

## Troubleshooting Common Issues

### Problem: Peppers getting soft
**Solution:** Move to cooler spot, use quickly

### Problem: Freezer burn
**Solution:** Double-bag and remove all air

### Problem: Bitter taste
**Solution:** Remove all seeds and white membranes

### Problem: Uneven cooking
**Solution:** Cut into uniform sizes

## Safety Notes
- Always wash before eating or cooking
- Discard peppers with mold immediately
- Use clean utensils and cutting boards
- Store cut peppers below 40°F
- Don't leave at room temperature over 2 hours
