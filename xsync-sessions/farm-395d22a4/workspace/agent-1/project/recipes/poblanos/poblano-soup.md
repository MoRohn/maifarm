# Creamy Roasted Poblano Soup

## Ingredients
- 6 poblano peppers
- 2 tbsp olive oil
- 1 large onion, diced
- 4 cloves garlic, minced
- 4 cups chicken or vegetable broth
- 1 cup heavy cream
- 2 medium potatoes, peeled and diced
- 1 tsp ground cumin
- 1/2 tsp smoked paprika
- Salt and pepper to taste
- 1/4 cup fresh cilantro
- Juice of 1 lime

### For garnish:
- Sour cream
- Tortilla strips
- Queso fresco
- Pumpkin seeds
- Fresh cilantro

## Instructions

1. **Roast poblanos**: Char poblanos over open flame or under broiler until blackened. Place in bag to steam for 10 minutes, then peel, seed, and chop.

2. **Sauté aromatics**: In large pot, heat olive oil over medium heat. Add onion and cook until translucent (5 minutes). Add garlic and cook 1 minute.

3. **Add vegetables**: Add chopped poblanos and diced potatoes. Cook for 5 minutes, stirring occasionally.

4. **Add liquid and spices**: Pour in broth, add cumin and paprika. Bring to boil, then simmer for 20 minutes until potatoes are tender.

5. **Blend soup**: Using immersion blender (or in batches in regular blender), puree soup until smooth.

6. **Add cream**: Stir in heavy cream and heat through. Season with salt and pepper.

7. **Finish**: Add cilantro and lime juice just before serving.

8. **Serve**: Ladle into bowls and garnish with desired toppings.

## Cooking Time
- Prep: 20 minutes
- Cook: 40 minutes
- Total: 1 hour

## Servings: 6

## Variations
- **Corn addition**: Add 1 cup roasted corn kernels
- **Protein boost**: Top with grilled shrimp or chicken
- **Vegan version**: Use coconut milk instead of cream

## Tips
- Soup can be made 2 days ahead and reheated
- Freeze for up to 3 months
- Adjust heat by keeping some poblano seeds
