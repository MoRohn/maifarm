# Classic Chiles Rellenos

## Ingredients

### For the peppers:
- 6 large poblano peppers
- 12 oz Oaxaca or Monterey Jack cheese, cut into strips
- All-purpose flour for dusting

### For the batter:
- 6 large eggs, separated
- 1/2 tsp salt
- Vegetable oil for frying

### For the sauce:
- 4 large tomatoes
- 1/2 white onion
- 3 cloves garlic
- 1 tsp salt
- 1/2 tsp cumin
- 2 cups chicken or vegetable broth

## Instructions

1. **Roast poblanos**: Char poblanos directly over gas flame or under broiler, turning frequently until skin is blackened all over (10-15 minutes).

2. **Steam and peel**: Place in plastic bag for 10 minutes to steam. Gently peel off skin, keeping peppers intact.

3. **Prepare peppers**: Make a small slit down one side and carefully remove seeds, keeping stem attached.

4. **Stuff peppers**: Insert cheese strips into each pepper. Close opening with toothpicks. Dust lightly with flour.

5. **Make sauce**: Blend roasted tomatoes, onion, garlic, salt, and cumin. Simmer in a pot with broth for 15 minutes.

6. **Prepare batter**: Beat egg whites with salt until stiff peaks form. Gently fold in beaten egg yolks.

7. **Heat oil**: Heat 1 inch of oil in large skillet to 375°F (190°C).

8. **Coat and fry**: Dip each pepper in batter, coating completely. Fry 3-4 minutes per side until golden brown.

9. **Serve**: Place on plates, ladle warm sauce over top. Serve immediately.

## Cooking Time
- Prep: 45 minutes
- Cook: 30 minutes
- Total: 1 hour 15 minutes

## Servings: 6

## Variations
- **Meat filling**: Use seasoned ground beef or shredded chicken with cheese
- **Baked version**: Skip batter, top with sauce and cheese, bake at 350°F for 20 minutes
- **Seafood filling**: Use shrimp and cream cheese mixture

## Tips
- Choose poblanos that are straight and smooth for easier stuffing
- Can roast and peel peppers a day ahead
- Keep oil temperature consistent for best results
