# Green Pepper Infused Vinegar

## Ingredients
- 2 cups white wine vinegar or apple cider vinegar
- 4 green bell peppers, thinly sliced
- 6 jalapeño peppers, sliced
- 2 serrano peppers, sliced (optional)
- 4 cloves garlic, crushed
- 2 tbsp sugar
- 1 tbsp salt
- 1 tsp mustard seeds
- 1 tsp coriander seeds
- Fresh herbs (thyme, oregano, or basil)

## Instructions

1. **Sterilize bottles**: Sterilize glass bottles or jars with boiling water and let dry completely.

2. **Prepare peppers**: Wash and slice all peppers. For a milder vinegar, remove seeds from hot peppers.

3. **Heat vinegar**: In a non-reactive pot, heat vinegar with sugar and salt until dissolved. Do not boil.

4. **Add aromatics**: Add mustard seeds, coriander seeds, and garlic to the warm vinegar.

5. **Pack bottles**: Divide peppers and herbs among bottles, filling about 1/3 full.

6. **Pour vinegar**: Pour warm vinegar mixture over peppers, leaving 1/2 inch headspace.

7. **Remove air bubbles**: Tap bottles gently to release air bubbles.

8. **Seal and age**: Seal bottles and store in a cool, dark place for 2-3 weeks before using.

9. **Strain (optional)**: After aging, strain out solids for a clear vinegar, or leave for continued infusion.

## Storage
- Unopened: 1 year in cool, dark place
- Opened: 6 months refrigerated

## Uses
- Salad dressings and vinaigrettes
- Pickling liquid for vegetables
- Marinade for chicken and fish
- Hot sauce base
- Deglazing pan sauces
- Splash in soups and stews
- Bloody Mary cocktails

## Flavor Variations
- **Sweet and spicy**: Add pineapple chunks and increase sugar
- **Herb forward**: Add extra basil, cilantro, or dill
- **Citrus twist**: Add lemon or lime zest
- **Smoky**: Use chipotle peppers instead of jalapeños

## Gift Giving Tips
- Use decorative bottles
- Add a sprig of fresh herbs for presentation
- Include recipe cards for using the vinegar
- Tie with raffia or ribbon
