# Green Pepper Infused Oil

## Ingredients
- 2 cups extra virgin olive oil or neutral oil
- 6-8 green bell peppers, chopped
- 3-4 jalapeños, sliced (optional for heat)
- 4 cloves garlic
- 2 tsp black peppercorns
- 1 tsp sea salt
- 2 sprigs fresh rosemary
- 2 bay leaves

## Instructions

### Cold Infusion Method:
1. **Prepare peppers**: Wash and thoroughly dry all peppers. Chop bell peppers, slice jalapeños.

2. **Combine ingredients**: In a sterilized jar, combine peppers, garlic, peppercorns, salt, and herbs.

3. **Add oil**: Pour oil over ingredients, ensuring everything is submerged.

4. **Infuse**: Seal jar and store in a cool, dark place for 2-4 weeks, shaking daily.

5. **Strain**: Strain through cheesecloth or fine mesh strainer. Transfer to clean bottles.

### Heat Infusion Method (Faster):
1. **Heat gently**: In a saucepan, combine oil and all ingredients. Heat to 180°F (82°C).

2. **Maintain temperature**: Keep at this temperature for 30 minutes. Do not let it smoke.

3. **Cool and steep**: Remove from heat, let cool completely. Transfer to jar and steep for 24 hours.

4. **Strain and bottle**: Strain and transfer to sterilized bottles.

## Storage
- Cold infused: 6 months in cool, dark place
- Heat infused: 3 months refrigerated
- Always use clean, dry utensils

## Uses
- Drizzle over grilled vegetables
- Pizza and pasta finishing oil
- Salad dressings
- Bread dipping
- Marinades for meat and seafood
- Sautéing base

## Variations
- **Asian style**: Add ginger, lemongrass, and Thai basil
- **Mediterranean**: Add sun-dried tomatoes and oregano
- **Smoky**: Use roasted peppers instead of fresh

## Safety Tips
- Always use completely dry ingredients to prevent botulism
- Store in refrigerator for longer shelf life
- Label with date of preparation
