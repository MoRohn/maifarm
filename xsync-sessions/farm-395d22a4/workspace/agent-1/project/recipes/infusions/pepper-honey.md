# Hot Pepper Infused Honey

## Ingredients
- 2 cups raw honey
- 4-6 jalapeño peppers, sliced
- 2 serrano peppers, sliced (optional for extra heat)
- 1 green bell pepper, finely diced (for flavor complexity)
- 2 cloves garlic, sliced thin
- 1 inch fresh ginger, sliced (optional)
- 1 tsp black pepper
- Zest of 1 lime

## Instructions

### Method 1: Cold Infusion
1. **Prepare peppers**: Wash, dry, and slice peppers. Remove seeds for milder honey.

2. **Layer in jar**: In a sterilized jar, layer peppers, garlic, and ginger.

3. **Add honey**: Pour honey over ingredients, stirring to remove air pockets.

4. **Infuse**: Seal and let sit at room temperature for 5-7 days, turning jar daily.

5. **Strain**: Strain through fine mesh, pressing solids to extract flavor.

### Method 2: Warm Infusion
1. **Warm honey**: Gently heat honey to 110°F (43°C) - do not overheat.

2. **Add ingredients**: Add peppers, garlic, and spices to warm honey.

3. **Steep**: Maintain temperature for 20 minutes, stirring occasionally.

4. **Cool and age**: Let cool, transfer to jar, age for 48 hours.

5. **Strain and store**: Strain and transfer to clean jars.

## Storage
- Room temperature: 6 months
- Refrigerated: 1 year
- Keep in airtight container

## Serving Suggestions
- Drizzle over pizza and flatbreads
- Glaze for grilled chicken or salmon
- Mix into salad dressings
- Cheese board accompaniment
- Cocktail ingredient (whiskey sours, margaritas)
- Toast or biscuit topping
- BBQ sauce base
- Tea sweetener for cold relief

## Heat Levels
- **Mild**: Use only bell peppers with 1-2 jalapeños
- **Medium**: Recipe as written
- **Hot**: Add habaneros or ghost peppers
- **Volcanic**: Include Carolina Reaper (use sparingly!)

## Variations
- **Tropical**: Add pineapple and coconut
- **Herbaceous**: Include rosemary or thyme
- **Smoky**: Use chipotle peppers
- **Citrus**: Add orange zest with lime

## Tips
- Always taste-test before straining
- Intensity increases over time
- Great homemade gift idea
- Use local honey for best flavor
