# Italian Green Pepper and Egg Scramble

## Ingredients
- 3 large green bell peppers, sliced
- 6 large eggs
- 1/4 cup whole milk
- 3 tbsp olive oil
- 1 medium onion, sliced
- 2 cloves garlic, minced
- 1/2 tsp salt
- 1/4 tsp black pepper
- 1/4 tsp red pepper flakes (optional)
- 1/2 cup grated Parmesan cheese
- Fresh basil leaves for garnish
- Italian bread for serving

## Instructions

1. **Prepare ingredients**: Slice peppers into 1/2-inch strips. Beat eggs with milk, salt, and pepper in a bowl.

2. **Cook peppers**: Heat olive oil in a large skillet over medium heat. Add peppers and onion. Cook for 10-12 minutes until peppers are soft and slightly charred.

3. **Add garlic**: Add minced garlic and red pepper flakes. Cook for 1 minute.

4. **Add eggs**: Reduce heat to low. Pour beaten eggs over the pepper mixture.

5. **Scramble gently**: Using a spatula, gently scramble eggs with peppers, moving them around the pan until eggs are just set but still creamy.

6. **Add cheese**: Remove from heat and sprinkle with Parmesan cheese.

7. **Serve**: Garnish with fresh basil and serve immediately with crusty Italian bread.

## Cooking Time
- Prep: 10 minutes
- Cook: 15 minutes
- Total: 25 minutes

## Servings: 4

## Variations
- **Breakfast sandwich**: Serve in a toasted Italian roll
- **Frittata style**: Pour eggs over peppers and bake in oven at 350°F for 15 minutes
- **Add protein**: Include cooked Italian sausage or pancetta

## Tips
- Don't overcook the eggs - they should be creamy
- This makes an excellent brunch dish
- Leftover pepper and egg can be used as a sandwich filling
