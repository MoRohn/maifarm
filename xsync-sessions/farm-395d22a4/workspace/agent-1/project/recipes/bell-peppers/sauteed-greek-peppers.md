# Sautéed Greek Green Peppers (Prasines Piperies)

## Ingredients
- 2 lbs green bell peppers, cut into strips
- 1 large red onion, sliced
- 4 tbsp extra virgin olive oil
- 3 cloves garlic, minced
- 1/4 cup fresh parsley, chopped
- 2 tsp dried oregano
- 1 tsp salt
- 1/2 tsp black pepper
- 2 tbsp red wine vinegar
- 1/2 cup crumbled feta cheese (optional)
- Crusty bread for serving

## Instructions

1. **Prepare vegetables**: Wash and dry peppers. Cut into 1-inch strips, removing seeds and membranes. Slice onion into half-moons.

2. **Heat the oil**: In a large frying pan or skillet, heat olive oil over medium heat.

3. **Sauté onions**: Add red onion and cook until softened and slightly caramelized, about 5-7 minutes.

4. **Add peppers**: Add pepper strips to the pan. Cook, stirring regularly, for 10-12 minutes until peppers are tender but still have a slight bite.

5. **Season**: Add garlic, oregano, salt, and pepper. Cook for 2 more minutes until fragrant.

6. **Add fresh herbs**: Stir in fresh parsley and red wine vinegar. Cook for 1 minute.

7. **Serve**: Transfer to a serving dish. Top with crumbled feta if desired. Serve warm or at room temperature with crusty bread.

## Cooking Time
- Prep: 15 minutes
- Cook: 20 minutes
- Total: 35 minutes

## Servings: 6 as a side dish

## Variations
- **Spicy version**: Add 1-2 sliced jalapeños
- **Tomato addition**: Add 2 chopped tomatoes in the last 5 minutes
- **Protein boost**: Add chickpeas or white beans

## Tips
- This dish is excellent served at room temperature as part of a mezze platter
- Can be made ahead and stored in the refrigerator for up to 5 days
- Use a mix of green, red, and yellow peppers for a colorful presentation
