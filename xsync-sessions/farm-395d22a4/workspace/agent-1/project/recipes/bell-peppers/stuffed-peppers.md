# Classic Stuffed Green Bell Peppers

## Ingredients
- 6 large green bell peppers, tops cut and seeds removed
- 1 lb ground beef (or turkey)
- 1 cup cooked white rice
- 1 medium onion, finely diced
- 3 cloves garlic, minced
- 1 can (14.5 oz) diced tomatoes, drained
- 1 cup tomato sauce
- 1 tsp Italian seasoning
- 1 tsp salt
- 1/2 tsp black pepper
- 1 1/2 cups shredded mozzarella cheese
- 2 tbsp olive oil
- Fresh parsley for garnish

## Instructions

1. **Prepare the peppers**: Preheat oven to 375°F (190°C). Cut tops off peppers and remove seeds and membranes. If needed, trim bottoms slightly so peppers stand upright. Brush with olive oil and place in a baking dish.

2. **Make the filling**: In a large skillet, heat olive oil over medium heat. Add onion and cook until softened (5 minutes). Add garlic and cook 1 minute more.

3. **Cook the meat**: Add ground beef, breaking it up with a spoon. Cook until browned and no longer pink. Season with salt and pepper.

4. **Combine ingredients**: Stir in cooked rice, diced tomatoes, half the tomato sauce, Italian seasoning, and 1 cup of cheese. Mix well and adjust seasoning.

5. **Stuff the peppers**: Fill each pepper with the meat and rice mixture. Top with remaining cheese.

6. **Bake**: Pour remaining tomato sauce around peppers in the dish. Cover with foil and bake for 35-40 minutes. Remove foil and bake 10 more minutes until cheese is golden.

7. **Serve**: Let cool for 5 minutes. Garnish with fresh parsley and serve.

## Cooking Time
- Prep: 20 minutes
- Cook: 50 minutes
- Total: 1 hour 10 minutes

## Servings: 6

## Tips
- For a spicier version, add diced jalapeños to the filling
- Can be made ahead and refrigerated for up to 2 days before baking
- Freeze unbaked stuffed peppers for up to 3 months
