# Bacon-Wrapped Jalapeño Poppers

## Ingredients
- 20 large jalapeño peppers
- 8 oz cream cheese, softened
- 1 cup shredded sharp cheddar cheese
- 1/2 cup shredded Monterey Jack cheese
- 1 tsp garlic powder
- 1/2 tsp onion powder
- 1/2 tsp smoked paprika
- 1/4 tsp cayenne pepper (optional)
- 20 strips bacon, cut in half
- Toothpicks

## Instructions

1. **Prepare jalapeños**: Preheat oven to 400°F (200°C). Cut jalapeños in half lengthwise. Remove seeds and membranes (wear gloves!).

2. **Make filling**: In a bowl, mix cream cheese, cheddar, Monterey Jack, garlic powder, onion powder, smoked paprika, and cayenne until well combined.

3. **Fill peppers**: Using a small spoon, fill each jalapeño half with cheese mixture, mounding slightly.

4. **Wrap with bacon**: Wrap each filled jalapeño with half a bacon strip. Secure with toothpick if needed.

5. **Arrange for baking**: Place on a baking sheet lined with parchment paper or a wire rack.

6. **Bake**: Bake for 20-25 minutes until bacon is crispy and peppers are tender.

7. **Cool and serve**: Let cool for 5 minutes before serving.

## Cooking Time
- Prep: 25 minutes
- Cook: 25 minutes
- Total: 50 minutes

## Servings: 40 poppers

## Variations
- **Grilled version**: Cook on grill over medium heat for 15-20 minutes
- **Air fryer**: Cook at 370°F for 12-15 minutes
- **Meat filling**: Add cooked crumbled sausage or ground beef to cheese mixture

## Tips
- For less heat, soak jalapeños in cold water for 30 minutes after cutting
- Can be assembled ahead and refrigerated for up to 24 hours before baking
- Save seeds to add heat to other dishes
