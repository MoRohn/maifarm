# Quick Pickled Jalapeños

## Ingredients
- 1 lb fresh jalapeño peppers
- 1 cup white vinegar
- 1 cup water
- 2 tbsp sugar
- 1 tbsp salt
- 3 cloves garlic, sliced
- 1 tsp black peppercorns
- 1 tsp coriander seeds
- 1/2 tsp cumin seeds
- 2 bay leaves
- 1 small onion, sliced (optional)
- 1 carrot, sliced (optional)

## Instructions

1. **Prepare jalapeños**: Wash jalapeños and slice into 1/4-inch rounds. For less heat, remove seeds.

2. **Prepare jars**: Sterilize 2 pint jars or 1 quart jar with boiling water.

3. **Make brine**: In a saucepan, combine vinegar, water, sugar, and salt. Bring to a boil, stirring to dissolve sugar and salt.

4. **Add aromatics**: Add garlic, peppercorns, coriander seeds, cumin seeds, and bay leaves to the brine. Simmer for 2 minutes.

5. **Pack jars**: Pack jalapeño slices (and optional onion and carrot) into jars.

6. **Pour brine**: Pour hot brine over jalapeños, ensuring they're completely covered. Leave 1/2 inch headspace.

7. **Remove air bubbles**: Tap jars gently to remove air bubbles. Add more brine if needed.

8. **Cool and refrigerate**: Let cool to room temperature, then refrigerate. Best after 24 hours.

## Storage Time
- Refrigerated: Up to 2 months
- For longer storage, process in water bath for 10 minutes

## Servings: Makes about 2 pints

## Variations
- **Mexican escabeche**: Add cauliflower florets and Mexican oregano
- **Sweet and spicy**: Increase sugar to 1/4 cup, add pineapple juice
- **Asian-inspired**: Use rice vinegar, add ginger and star anise

## Uses
- Tacos and nachos
- Sandwiches and burgers
- Pizza topping
- Bloody Marys
- Chopped in salads and salsas

## Tips
- Wear gloves when handling jalapeños
- Save the pickling liquid to use in salad dressings
- Add a grape leaf to keep peppers crisp
