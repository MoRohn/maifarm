# Jalapeño Cheddar Cornbread

## Ingredients
- 1 cup yellow cornmeal
- 1 cup all-purpose flour
- 1/4 cup sugar
- 1 tbsp baking powder
- 1 tsp salt
- 1 cup buttermilk
- 1/3 cup vegetable oil
- 2 large eggs
- 1 1/2 cups shredded sharp cheddar cheese
- 3-4 jalapeños, seeded and diced
- 1/4 cup fresh or frozen corn kernels
- 2 tbsp honey
- 2 tbsp butter for greasing

## Instructions

1. **Preheat and prepare**: Preheat oven to 400°F (200°C). Grease a 9-inch cast iron skillet or 8x8 baking dish with butter.

2. **Mix dry ingredients**: In a large bowl, whisk together cornmeal, flour, sugar, baking powder, and salt.

3. **Combine wet ingredients**: In another bowl, whisk buttermilk, oil, eggs, and honey until well combined.

4. **Combine mixtures**: Pour wet ingredients into dry ingredients. Stir until just combined - don't overmix.

5. **Add mix-ins**: Fold in 1 cup cheddar cheese, diced jalapeños, and corn kernels.

6. **Pour and top**: Pour batter into prepared pan. Sprinkle remaining cheese on top.

7. **Bake**: Bake for 20-25 minutes until golden brown and a toothpick comes out clean.

8. **Cool and serve**: Let cool for 10 minutes. Serve warm with butter and honey.

## Cooking Time
- Prep: 15 minutes
- Cook: 25 minutes
- Total: 40 minutes

## Servings: 9

## Variations
- **Bacon addition**: Add 4 strips of cooked, crumbled bacon
- **Cream cheese swirl**: Drop spoonfuls of cream cheese into batter before baking
- **Mexican style**: Add 1 tsp cumin and 1/2 cup Mexican cheese blend

## Tips
- For extra moist cornbread, add 1/4 cup sour cream
- Adjust jalapeño quantity based on heat preference
- Leftovers make excellent croutons when cubed and toasted
