## Pepper Selection Tips

### Bell Peppers
- Choose firm, glossy peppers with taut skin
- Heavier peppers have thicker walls (better for stuffing)
- Store in refrigerator crisper for up to 2 weeks

### Jalapeños
- Red jalapeños are riper and slightly sweeter
- Stretch marks indicate more heat
- Smooth skin usually means milder peppers

### Poblanos
- Dark green, almost black color when ripe
- Should be firm and glossy
- Larger size makes stuffing easier

## Safety Tips

⚠️ **When handling hot peppers:**
- Always wear gloves
- Don't touch eyes or face
- Wash hands thoroughly with dish soap
- Use milk or yogurt to neutralize capsaicin burn

## Storage Guidelines

### Fresh Peppers
- **Bell peppers**: Refrigerate up to 2 weeks
- **Jalapeños**: Refrigerate up to 1 week
- **Poblanos**: Refrigerate up to 1 week

### Prepared Items
- **Infused oils**: Cool, dark place for 3-6 months
- **Infused vinegars**: Pantry for 1 year
- **Infused honey**: Room temperature for 6 months
- **Pickled peppers**: Refrigerated for 2 months

- **Fiber** - Digestive health
