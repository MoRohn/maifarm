## Meal Prep Tips

### Sunday Prep Session
1. **Cook grains in bulk**: Prepare brown rice and quinoa for the week
2. **Chop vegetables**: Pre-cut vegetables for quick cooking
3. **Marinate proteins**: Prepare marinades for chicken and tofu
4. **Prepare snacks**: Portion out nuts, cut fruit, make hummus

### Storage Guidelines
- **Cooked grains**: 4-5 days in refrigerator
- **Chopped vegetables**: 3-4 days in airtight containers
- **Cooked proteins**: 3-4 days in refrigerator
- **Prepared salads**: Keep dressing separate until serving

### Time-Saving Strategies
1. Use one-pan or sheet-pan recipes
2. Double recipes for leftovers
3. Prep ingredients while others cook
4. Use frozen vegetables when needed
5. Keep pantry stocked with essentials

## Nutritional Balance

### Daily Goals
- **Protein**: 25-30% of calories
- **Carbohydrates**: 45-50% of calories
- **Fats**: 25-30% of calories
- **Fiber**: 25-35 grams
- **Vegetables**: 5-7 servings

### Portion Guidelines
- **Protein**: Palm-sized portion (3-4 oz)
- **Grains**: Fist-sized portion (1/2 cup cooked)
- **Vegetables**: 2 fist-sized portions
- **Healthy fats**: Thumb-sized portion

## Budget Tips

### Money-Saving Strategies
1. Buy proteins in bulk and freeze portions
2. Choose seasonal vegetables
3. Use dried beans and lentils
4. Plan meals around sales
5. Minimize food waste by using leftovers

### Cost-Effective Proteins
- Eggs ($2-3/dozen)
- Dried beans/lentils ($1-2/lb)
- Chicken thighs ($3-4/lb)
- Canned fish ($2-4/can)
- Tofu ($2-3/package)
