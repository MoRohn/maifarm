## 📝 Meal Ideas with These Ingredients

### Tuesday: Turkey & Veggie Stir-Fry

### Wednesday: Mediterranean Quinoa Bowl

### Thursday: Chicken & Vegetable Curry
- Chicken breast + cauliflower + spinach + coconut milk + curry spices

### Friday: Black Bean & Sweet Potato Tacos

### Sunday: Buddha Bowl
- Brown rice + tofu + kale + carrots + avocado + tahini dressing

## 💡 Shopping Tips
1. Buy seasonal vegetables for better prices and freshness
2. Prep vegetables on Sunday for easier weeknight cooking
3. Freeze extra portions for quick future meals
4. Check for sales on proteins and stock up
5. Store herbs with stems in water to keep fresh longer

## 🥗 Prep-Ahead Ideas
- Wash and chop vegetables
- Cook grains in bulk
- Marinate proteins
- Make salad dressings
- Prepare overnight oats for breakfast
