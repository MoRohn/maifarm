The Golden Tower

A man of towers reaching high,
Gold-lettered name against the sky,
From Queens to Manhattan's gleaming shore,
Built an empire, always wanting more.

The apprentice turned to center stage,
Reality TV in a golden age,
"You're fired!" echoed through the land,
As business merged with entertainment's brand.

From boardroom to the campaign trail,
Breaking norms that once prevailed,
Twitter storms at break of dawn,
Traditional politics withdrawn.

Red cap worn by millions strong,
"Make it great" became the song,
Rallies filled with fervent crowd,
Supporters passionate and loud.

The 45th to take the oath,
Dividing some while inspiring both,
A presidency unlike before,
Controversy at every door.

Through impeachments, trials, and media fights,
Defending what he saw as rights,
Never backing down from fray,
Living life the Trump-branded way.

Mar-a-Lago's palm-lined grounds,
Where politics and golf resounds,
Still commanding headlines bright,
In America's political sight.

Love him, hate him, in between,
Few figures such as Trump we've seen,
A chapter written bold and loud,
In history's ever-turning cloud.
