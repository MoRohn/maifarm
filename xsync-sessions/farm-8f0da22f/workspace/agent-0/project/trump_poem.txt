The Golden Tower

A man of towers, golden gleam,
From Queens he rose to chase his dream,
With bold ambition, never small,
His name emblazoned on the wall.

The boardroom king with pointed hand,
"You're fired!" echoed through the land,
From real estate to TV fame,
A household figure, known by name.

The escalator brought him down,
To seek the highest office crown,
With Twitter storms and rallies loud,
He drew both critics and his crowd.

The forty-fifth to take the oath,
Dividing some while loved by both,
His legacy, a complex tale,
Of triumph, controversy, and travail.

Through Mar-a-Lago's palm-lined shore,
To courtroom battles by the score,
A figure bold, beyond compare,
With golden tower and golden hair.

History will long debate,
This man who stirred such love and hate,
A chapter written, bold and bright,
In America's ongoing fight.
