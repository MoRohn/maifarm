# Green Pepper Sauces & Condiments

Transform green peppers into versatile sauces, relishes, and condiments that enhance any meal.

---

## 🌶️ Roasted Green Pepper Sauce

### Overview
**Prep Time:** 15 minutes  
**Cook Time:** 30 minutes  
**Yield:** 2 cups  
**Difficulty:** Easy  
**Storage:** 2 weeks refrigerated

A smoky, versatile sauce perfect for pasta, grilled meats, or as a sandwich spread.

### Ingredients
- 6 large green bell peppers
- 4 cloves garlic, peeled
- ½ cup extra virgin olive oil
- 2 tablespoons sherry vinegar
- 1 teaspoon smoked paprika
- ½ teaspoon ground cumin
- Salt and black pepper to taste
- 2 tablespoons fresh parsley
- 1 tablespoon honey (optional, for balance)

### Instructions
1. **Roast the peppers:**
   - Place whole peppers on a baking sheet.
   - Broil, turning frequently, until charred all over (15-20 minutes).
   - Transfer to a bowl, cover with plastic wrap, let steam for 10 minutes.
   - Peel off skins, remove stems and seeds.

2. **Make the sauce:**
   - In a food processor, combine roasted peppers and garlic.
   - With motor running, slowly drizzle in olive oil.
   - Add vinegar, paprika, cumin, salt, and pepper.
   - Pulse in parsley.
   - Taste and adjust seasoning, add honey if desired.

3. **Storage:**
   - Transfer to sterilized jar.
   - Cover surface with thin layer of olive oil.
   - Refrigerate for up to 2 weeks.

### Uses
- Pasta sauce (thin with pasta water)
- Pizza base
- Sandwich spread
- Marinade for chicken or fish
- Dip for vegetables or bread

## 🥫 Green Pepper Relish

### Overview
**Prep Time:** 30 minutes  
**Cook Time:** 45 minutes  
**Yield:** 4 cups  
**Difficulty:** Medium  
**Storage:** 1 year canned, 1 month refrigerated

A tangy-sweet condiment perfect for hot dogs, burgers, and sandwiches.

### Ingredients
- 12 large green bell peppers, finely chopped
- 4 medium onions, finely chopped
- 2 cups apple cider vinegar
- 1½ cups sugar
- 2 tablespoons salt
- 2 tablespoons mustard seeds
- 1 tablespoon celery seeds
- 1 teaspoon turmeric
- ½ teaspoon red pepper flakes

### Instructions
1. **Prepare vegetables:**
   - Finely chop peppers and onions (food processor works well).
   - Place in colander, sprinkle with salt.
   - Let drain for 2 hours, then rinse and squeeze dry.

2. **Cook relish:**
   - In large pot, combine vinegar, sugar, and all spices.
   - Bring to a boil, stirring to dissolve sugar.
   - Add drained vegetables.
   - Simmer for 30 minutes, stirring occasionally.

3. **Can or store:**
   - For canning: Fill sterilized jars, leaving ½ inch headspace.
   - Process in boiling water bath for 10 minutes.
   - For refrigerator: Cool completely and store in airtight containers.

### Serving Suggestions
- Top hot dogs and sausages
- Mix into tuna or chicken salad
- Add to deviled eggs
- Serve with cheese and crackers
- Stir into potato salad

## 🌿 Green Pepper Pesto

### Overview
**Prep Time:** 10 minutes  
**Yield:** 1½ cups  
**Difficulty:** Easy  
**Storage:** 1 week refrigerated, 3 months frozen

A unique twist on traditional pesto using roasted green peppers.

### Ingredients
- 2 large green bell peppers, roasted and peeled
- 1 cup fresh basil leaves
- ½ cup toasted pine nuts (or walnuts)
- 3 cloves garlic
- ½ cup Parmesan cheese, grated
- ⅔ cup extra virgin olive oil
- Juice of ½ lemon
- Salt and black pepper to taste

### Instructions
1. Roast peppers until charred, peel and seed.
2. In food processor, combine peppers, basil, nuts, and garlic.
3. Pulse until coarsely chopped.
4. Add Parmesan and lemon juice.
5. With motor running, slowly add olive oil until desired consistency.
6. Season with salt and pepper.

### Variations
- **Vegan:** Replace Parmesan with nutritional yeast
- **Spicy:** Add 1-2 jalapeños
- **Herb variation:** Use cilantro instead of basil

### Uses
- Toss with pasta
- Spread on sandwiches
- Mix into mayonnaise for aioli
- Drizzle over grilled vegetables
- Use as pizza sauce

## 🌎 Puerto Rican Sofrito (Green Pepper Base)

### Overview
**Prep Time:** 15 minutes  
**Yield:** 3 cups  
**Difficulty:** Easy  
**Storage:** 2 weeks refrigerated, 6 months frozen

The essential flavor base for Latin Caribbean cooking.

### Ingredients
- 3 large green bell peppers, roughly chopped
- 1 large red bell pepper, roughly chopped
- 2 medium onions, quartered
- 1 head garlic (10-12 cloves), peeled
- 1 bunch cilantro (about 1 cup packed)
- 1 bunch culantro/recao (or extra cilantro)
- 6 ají dulce peppers (or 1 additional bell pepper)
- 3 tablespoons olive oil
- 1 teaspoon salt
- ½ teaspoon oregano

### Instructions
1. Combine all ingredients in food processor.
2. Process until finely minced but not puréed.
3. Transfer to glass jars.
4. Cover surface with thin layer of olive oil.
5. Store refrigerated or freeze in ice cube trays.

### Traditional Uses
- Sauté 2-3 tablespoons as base for:
  - Arroz con gandules (rice with pigeon peas)
  - Beans of any kind
  - Soups and stews
  - Meat marinades

## 🔥 Fermented Green Pepper Hot Sauce

### Overview
**Prep Time:** 20 minutes  
**Fermentation Time:** 1-2 weeks  
**Yield:** 2 cups  
**Difficulty:** Medium  
**Storage:** 6 months refrigerated

A tangy, probiotic-rich hot sauce with complex flavors.

### Ingredients
- 10 green bell peppers, chopped
- 6 jalapeño peppers, chopped
- 6 cloves garlic
- 3% salt brine (30g salt per liter of water)
- 2 tablespoons sugar
- ¼ cup white vinegar (after fermentation)

### Instructions
1. **Set up fermentation:**
   - Pack peppers and garlic in clean jar.
   - Cover with salt brine, leaving 1 inch headspace.
   - Use fermentation weight to keep submerged.
   - Cover with coffee filter and rubber band.

2. **Ferment:**
   - Leave at room temperature for 7-14 days.
   - Check daily, skim any white film.
   - Taste after 7 days for desired tanginess.

3. **Finish sauce:**
   - Strain peppers, reserve brine.
   - Blend peppers with ½ cup brine and vinegar.
   - Strain if desired for smoother sauce.
   - Bottle and refrigerate.

### Flavor Notes
- Tangy and complex
- Less acidic than vinegar-based sauces
- Probiotic benefits
- Mellows over time

## 🍯 Sweet Green Pepper Jam

### Overview
**Prep Time:** 20 minutes  
**Cook Time:** 30 minutes  
**Yield:** 3 cups  
**Difficulty:** Medium  
**Storage:** 1 year sealed, 1 month opened

A sweet-savory jam perfect with cheese, meats, or as a glaze.

### Ingredients
- 8 large green bell peppers, finely chopped
- 2 jalapeño peppers, minced (optional)
- 1½ cups apple cider vinegar
- 6 cups sugar
- 1 package (3 oz) liquid pectin
- ½ teaspoon salt
- Green food coloring (optional)

### Instructions
1. Process peppers in food processor until finely minced.
2. In large pot, combine peppers, vinegar, and sugar.
3. Bring to rolling boil, stirring constantly.
4. Boil for 10 minutes.
5. Add pectin, return to boil for 1 minute.
6. Remove from heat, skim foam.
7. Add food coloring if desired.
8. Ladle into sterilized jars.
9. Process in water bath for 10 minutes.

### Serving Ideas
- Spread over cream cheese with crackers
- Glaze for roasted meats
- Mix into vinaigrettes
- Top brie or camembert
- Use as sandwich condiment

## 🥄 Green Pepper Chimichurri

### Overview
**Prep Time:** 10 minutes  
**Rest Time:** 30 minutes  
**Yield:** 1½ cups  
**Difficulty:** Easy  
**Storage:** 1 week refrigerated

An Argentinian-inspired sauce featuring green peppers.

### Ingredients
- 1 large green bell pepper, finely minced
- 1 cup fresh parsley, finely chopped
- ½ cup fresh cilantro, chopped
- 4 cloves garlic, minced
- ½ cup olive oil
- 3 tablespoons red wine vinegar
- 1 tablespoon fresh oregano
- 1 teaspoon red pepper flakes
- Salt and black pepper to taste

### Instructions
1. Combine all ingredients in a bowl.
2. Let rest for 30 minutes for flavors to meld.
3. Taste and adjust seasoning.
4. Store in airtight container.

### Uses
- Marinade for grilled meats
- Topping for steaks
- Mix into mayonnaise
- Drizzle over roasted vegetables
- Stir into soups

## Storage & Preservation Tips

### Refrigeration
- Store in glass containers
- Cover surface with oil to prevent oxidation
- Label with date
- Use clean utensils to prevent contamination

### Freezing
- Freeze in ice cube trays for portions
- Transfer cubes to freezer bags
- Flatten sauces in bags for quick thawing
- Label with name and date

### Canning Safety
- Always use tested recipes for canning
- Check jar seals before storage
- Process for full recommended time
- Store in cool, dark place

### Fresh Storage
- Most fresh sauces keep 1-2 weeks
- Fermented sauces last longer (months)
- Watch for signs of spoilage
- When in doubt, throw it out
