# Quick & Easy Green Pepper Recipes

Fast, delicious recipes featuring green peppers that can be prepared in 30 minutes or less.

---

## ⚡ 15-Minute Green Pepper Stir-Fry

### Overview
**Prep Time:** 5 minutes  
**Cook Time:** 10 minutes  
**Servings:** 4  
**Difficulty:** Easy

### Ingredients
- 3 large green bell peppers, sliced
- 2 tablespoons vegetable oil
- 2 cloves garlic, minced
- 1 tablespoon soy sauce
- 1 teaspoon sesame oil
- 1 teaspoon cornstarch mixed with 2 tablespoons water
- Salt and pepper to taste
- Sesame seeds for garnish

### Instructions
1. Heat oil in wok or large skillet over high heat.
2. Add peppers, stir-fry for 3-4 minutes until slightly charred.
3. Add garlic, cook 30 seconds.
4. Add soy sauce and sesame oil.
5. Add cornstarch slurry, toss until glossy.
6. Serve immediately over rice.

## 🥗 Green Pepper and Chickpea Salad

### Overview
**Prep Time:** 10 minutes  
**No Cooking Required**  
**Servings:** 4  
**Difficulty:** Easy

### Ingredients
- 2 large green bell peppers, diced
- 1 can (15 oz) chickpeas, drained
- 1 cup cherry tomatoes, halved
- ½ red onion, thinly sliced
- ¼ cup olive oil
- 2 tablespoons lemon juice
- 1 teaspoon dried oregano
- ½ cup feta cheese, crumbled
- Salt and pepper to taste

### Instructions
1. Combine peppers, chickpeas, tomatoes, and onion in bowl.
2. Whisk together oil, lemon juice, oregano, salt, and pepper.
3. Pour dressing over vegetables.
4. Top with feta cheese.
5. Serve immediately or chill.

## 🌮 Green Pepper Quesadilla

### Overview
**Prep Time:** 5 minutes  
**Cook Time:** 8 minutes  
**Servings:** 2  
**Difficulty:** Easy

### Ingredients
- 1 large green bell pepper, thinly sliced
- 1 tablespoon oil
- 2 large flour tortillas
- 1 cup shredded cheese (cheddar or Mexican blend)
- ½ teaspoon cumin
- ½ teaspoon chili powder
- Sour cream and salsa for serving

### Instructions
1. Sauté peppers in oil for 3-4 minutes until softened.
2. Season with cumin and chili powder.
3. Place one tortilla in clean skillet.
4. Add cheese, peppers, more cheese, and second tortilla.
5. Cook 2-3 minutes per side until golden.
6. Cut into wedges and serve.

## 🍳 Green Pepper Scrambled Eggs

### Overview
**Prep Time:** 3 minutes  
**Cook Time:** 5 minutes  
**Servings:** 2  
**Difficulty:** Easy

### Ingredients
- 1 green bell pepper, diced small
- 4 eggs, beaten
- 2 tablespoons butter
- ¼ cup shredded cheese
- Salt and pepper to taste
- Toast for serving

### Instructions
1. Melt butter in skillet over medium heat.
2. Add diced peppers, cook 2 minutes.
3. Pour in beaten eggs.
4. Gently scramble until just set.
5. Add cheese, remove from heat.
6. Serve immediately on toast.

## 🍕 Green Pepper Flatbread Pizza

### Overview
**Prep Time:** 5 minutes  
**Cook Time:** 12 minutes  
**Servings:** 2  
**Difficulty:** Easy

### Ingredients
- 1 naan bread or flatbread
- ½ cup pizza sauce
- 1 green bell pepper, thinly sliced
- 1 cup mozzarella cheese
- ½ teaspoon Italian seasoning
- Red pepper flakes (optional)

### Instructions
1. Preheat oven to 425°F (220°C).
2. Spread sauce on flatbread.
3. Top with cheese and pepper slices.
4. Sprinkle with Italian seasoning.
5. Bake 10-12 minutes until cheese bubbles.
6. Slice and serve immediately.

## 🥙 Green Pepper Hummus Wrap

### Overview
**Prep Time:** 5 minutes  
**No Cooking Required**  
**Servings:** 1  
**Difficulty:** Easy

### Ingredients
- 1 large tortilla or wrap
- ¼ cup hummus
- 1 green bell pepper, julienned
- ¼ cucumber, sliced
- Handful of spinach
- 2 tablespoons crumbled feta
- Squeeze of lemon juice

### Instructions
1. Spread hummus on tortilla.
2. Layer with peppers, cucumber, and spinach.
3. Add feta and lemon juice.
4. Roll tightly, securing with toothpick.
5. Cut in half diagonally and serve.

## 🍜 Quick Green Pepper Soup

### Overview
**Prep Time:** 5 minutes  
**Cook Time:** 20 minutes  
**Servings:** 4  
**Difficulty:** Easy

### Ingredients
- 3 green bell peppers, chopped
- 1 onion, diced
- 2 cups vegetable broth
- 1 cup coconut milk
- 2 cloves garlic, minced
- 2 tablespoons olive oil
- Salt, pepper, and paprika to taste

### Instructions
1. Sauté onion in oil for 3 minutes.
2. Add peppers and garlic, cook 5 minutes.
3. Add broth, bring to boil.
4. Simmer 10 minutes until peppers are soft.
5. Blend until smooth.
6. Stir in coconut milk, season, and serve.

## 🍝 Green Pepper Pasta

### Overview
**Prep Time:** 5 minutes  
**Cook Time:** 15 minutes  
**Servings:** 4  
**Difficulty:** Easy

### Ingredients
- 12 oz pasta
- 2 green bell peppers, sliced
- 3 cloves garlic, minced
- ¼ cup olive oil
- ½ cup pasta water
- ½ cup Parmesan cheese
- Red pepper flakes
- Fresh basil

### Instructions
1. Cook pasta according to package directions.
2. Meanwhile, sauté peppers in olive oil for 5 minutes.
3. Add garlic, cook 1 minute.
4. Add cooked pasta and pasta water.
5. Toss with Parmesan and basil.
6. Serve immediately.

## 🥪 Grilled Green Pepper and Cheese Sandwich

### Overview
**Prep Time:** 5 minutes  
**Cook Time:** 8 minutes  
**Servings:** 2  
**Difficulty:** Easy

### Ingredients
- 4 slices bread
- 1 green bell pepper, sliced thin
- 4 slices cheese (cheddar or provolone)
- 2 tablespoons butter
- 1 tablespoon mustard (optional)

### Instructions
1. Butter outside of bread slices.
2. Layer cheese, peppers, and cheese between bread.
3. Grill in skillet over medium heat.
4. Cook 3-4 minutes per side until golden.
5. Serve immediately.

## Time-Saving Tips

### Prep Ahead
- Wash and slice peppers on Sunday for the week
- Store in airtight containers
- Pre-make spice blends
- Keep canned beans on hand

### One-Pan Meals
- Use same skillet for multiple components
- Build flavors in layers
- Clean as you go
- Use parchment for easy cleanup

### Quick Cooking Methods
- High heat for stir-fries
- Thin slicing for faster cooking
- Microwave to pre-soften
- Use pre-cooked proteins

### Kitchen Shortcuts
- Food processor for quick chopping
- Pre-shredded cheese
- Jarred sauces as base
- Rotisserie chicken for protein
