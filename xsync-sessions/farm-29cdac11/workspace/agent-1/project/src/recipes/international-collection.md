## 🇬🇷 Greek Sautéed Green Peppers (Prasines Piperies Tiganistes)

### Overview
**Prep Time:** 10 minutes  
**Cook Time:** 15 minutes  
**Servings:** 4-6  
**Difficulty:** Easy

A simple yet flavorful Greek side dish that transforms humble green peppers into a delicious accompaniment.

### Ingredients
- 2 lbs green bell peppers, cut into strips
- ½ cup extra virgin olive oil
- 1 large red onion, sliced
- 3 cloves garlic, minced
- 2 tablespoons red wine vinegar
- 1 teaspoon dried oregano
- ½ cup fresh parsley, chopped
- Salt and black pepper to taste
- Crumbled feta cheese (optional)

### Instructions
1. Heat olive oil in a large skillet over medium-high heat.
2. Add pepper strips and sauté for 5-7 minutes until slightly charred.
3. Add onion and cook for 3-4 minutes until softened.
4. Add garlic and oregano, cook for 1 minute.
5. Deglaze with red wine vinegar.
6. Season with salt and pepper.
7. Remove from heat, stir in parsley.
8. Serve warm or at room temperature, topped with feta if desired.

### Serving Suggestions
- Serve as a side with grilled meats
- Use as a topping for crusty bread
- Mix with pasta for a quick meal

## 🇲🇽 Mexican Green Pepper Fajitas

### Overview
**Prep Time:** 20 minutes  
**Cook Time:** 15 minutes  
**Servings:** 4  
**Difficulty:** Easy

Sizzling fajitas featuring green peppers as the star vegetable, perfect for a festive meal.

### Ingredients

#### For the Marinade
- ¼ cup lime juice
- ¼ cup olive oil
- 2 teaspoons ground cumin
- 1 teaspoon chili powder
- 1 teaspoon smoked paprika
- 3 cloves garlic, minced
- Salt and pepper to taste

#### For the Fajitas
- 1½ lbs chicken breast or steak, sliced thin
- 3 large green bell peppers, sliced
- 2 medium onions, sliced
- 2 jalapeño peppers, sliced (optional)
- 8-10 flour tortillas
- 2 tablespoons vegetable oil

#### Toppings
- Sour cream
- Guacamole
- Pico de gallo
- Shredded cheese
- Fresh cilantro
- Lime wedges

### Instructions
1. Combine marinade ingredients in a bowl.
2. Marinate meat for at least 30 minutes.
3. Heat oil in a large cast-iron skillet over high heat.
4. Cook meat until done, remove and keep warm.
5. In same skillet, sauté peppers and onions until charred but crisp.
6. Return meat to skillet, toss together.
7. Serve immediately with warm tortillas and toppings.

## 🇮🇹 Italian Peperonata

### Overview
**Prep Time:** 15 minutes  
**Cook Time:** 40 minutes  
**Servings:** 6  
**Difficulty:** Easy

A rustic Italian stew of green peppers, tomatoes, and onions - perfect as a side or pasta sauce.

### Ingredients
- 4 large green bell peppers, cut into strips
- 2 red bell peppers, cut into strips
- 2 large onions, sliced
- 4 ripe tomatoes, peeled and chopped
- ½ cup olive oil
- 3 cloves garlic, sliced
- 2 bay leaves
- 1 teaspoon sugar
- 2 tablespoons capers (optional)
- Fresh basil leaves
- Salt and black pepper

### Instructions
1. Heat olive oil in a large pan over medium heat.
2. Add onions and cook until translucent (5 minutes).
3. Add peppers and bay leaves, cook for 10 minutes.
4. Add garlic, cook for 1 minute.
5. Add tomatoes, sugar, salt, and pepper.
6. Simmer uncovered for 25-30 minutes until thick.
7. Stir in capers and basil before serving.

### Uses
- Serve with grilled meats or fish
- Toss with pasta
- Use as a pizza topping
- Serve on bruschetta

## 🇨🇳 Chinese Pepper Beef (Qing Jiao Niu Rou)

### Overview
**Prep Time:** 20 minutes  
**Cook Time:** 10 minutes  
**Servings:** 4  
**Difficulty:** Medium

A classic Sichuan dish featuring tender beef and crisp green peppers in a savory sauce.

### Ingredients

#### For the Beef
- 1 lb beef sirloin, sliced thin against grain
- 2 tablespoons soy sauce
- 1 tablespoon Shaoxing wine (or dry sherry)
- 1 tablespoon cornstarch
- 1 teaspoon sesame oil

#### For the Stir-Fry
- 3 large green bell peppers, cut into chunks
- 3 tablespoons vegetable oil
- 3 cloves garlic, minced
- 1 inch ginger, minced
- 2 green onions, chopped

#### For the Sauce
- 2 tablespoons soy sauce
- 1 tablespoon oyster sauce
- 1 teaspoon sugar
- ½ teaspoon white pepper
- ¼ cup beef broth
- 1 teaspoon cornstarch

### Instructions
1. Marinate beef with soy sauce, wine, cornstarch, and sesame oil for 15 minutes.
2. Mix sauce ingredients and set aside.
3. Heat wok over high heat with 2 tablespoons oil.
4. Stir-fry beef for 2 minutes until browned, remove.
5. Add remaining oil, stir-fry peppers for 2 minutes.
6. Add garlic and ginger, stir-fry 30 seconds.
7. Return beef to wok, add sauce.
8. Toss until sauce thickens (1 minute).
9. Garnish with green onions and serve with rice.

## 🇮🇳 Indian Green Pepper Curry (Shimla Mirch Sabzi)

### Overview
**Prep Time:** 15 minutes  
**Cook Time:** 25 minutes  
**Servings:** 4  
**Difficulty:** Medium

A flavorful vegetarian curry featuring green peppers in aromatic Indian spices.

### Ingredients
- 4 large green bell peppers, cut into squares
- 2 medium potatoes, cubed (optional)
- 2 tablespoons ghee or oil
- 1 teaspoon cumin seeds
- 1 large onion, chopped
- 2 tomatoes, chopped
- 1 tablespoon ginger-garlic paste
- 1 teaspoon turmeric powder
- 1 teaspoon coriander powder
- ½ teaspoon red chili powder
- 1 teaspoon garam masala
- ½ teaspoon amchur (dry mango powder)
- Salt to taste
- Fresh coriander for garnish

### Instructions
1. Heat ghee in a pan, add cumin seeds.
2. When seeds splutter, add onions and sauté until golden.
3. Add ginger-garlic paste, cook for 1 minute.
4. Add tomatoes and all dry spices except garam masala.
5. Cook until tomatoes break down into a paste.
6. Add peppers and potatoes (if using).
7. Cover and cook for 15 minutes, stirring occasionally.
8. Add garam masala and amchur.
9. Cook for 5 more minutes until peppers are tender.
10. Garnish with fresh coriander and serve with roti or rice.

## 🇵🇷 Puerto Rican Sofrito

### Overview
**Prep Time:** 15 minutes  
**Yield:** 2 cups  
**Difficulty:** Easy

The aromatic base for countless Puerto Rican dishes, featuring green peppers prominently.

### Ingredients
- 2 large green bell peppers, roughly chopped
- 1 red bell pepper, roughly chopped
- 2 medium onions, quartered
- 1 head of garlic, peeled
- 1 bunch cilantro
- 1 bunch culantro (or extra cilantro)
- 4-6 ají dulce peppers (or 1 more bell pepper)
- 2 tablespoons olive oil
- Salt to taste

### Instructions
1. Combine all ingredients in a food processor.
2. Pulse until finely chopped but not puréed.
3. Store in airtight container in refrigerator for up to 2 weeks.
4. Freeze in ice cube trays for longer storage.

### Uses
- Sauté 2-3 tablespoons as base for rice dishes
- Add to beans and stews
- Use as marinade for meats
- Mix into ground meat for extra flavor

## Tips for International Cooking with Green Peppers

### Selecting Peppers
- Different cuisines may call for different pepper sizes
- Younger, smaller peppers work better for quick stir-fries
- Larger, thicker peppers are ideal for stuffing or slow cooking

### Preparation Techniques
- **Charring:** Many cuisines benefit from charred pepper flavor
- **Blanching:** Softens peppers for certain preparations
- **Raw:** Some dishes use raw peppers for crunch

### Storage of Prepared Dishes
- Most dishes keep 3-4 days refrigerated
- Freeze portions for up to 2 months
- Reheat gently to maintain texture
