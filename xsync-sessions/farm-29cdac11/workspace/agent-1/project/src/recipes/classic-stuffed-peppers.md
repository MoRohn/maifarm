# Classic Stuffed Green Peppers

## Overview
**Prep Time:** 20 minutes  
**Cook Time:** 45 minutes  
**Total Time:** 1 hour 5 minutes  
**Servings:** 6  
**Difficulty:** Easy

A hearty, comforting dish featuring tender green bell peppers filled with a savory mixture of ground beef, rice, tomatoes, and cheese. Perfect for family dinners and meal prep.

## Ingredients

### For the Peppers
- 6 large green bell peppers, tops cut and seeds removed
- 1 tablespoon olive oil
- Salt and pepper to taste

### For the Filling
- 1 lb ground beef (85/15 lean)
- 1 medium onion, finely diced
- 3 cloves garlic, minced
- 1½ cups cooked white rice
- 1 can (14.5 oz) diced tomatoes, drained
- 1 can (8 oz) tomato sauce, divided
- 2 teaspoons Worcestershire sauce
- 1 teaspoon dried Italian seasoning
- ½ teaspoon smoked paprika
- Salt and black pepper to taste
- 1½ cups shredded mozzarella cheese, divided
- ¼ cup fresh parsley, chopped

### For the Sauce
- Remaining tomato sauce
- ½ cup beef broth
- 1 tablespoon tomato paste
- ½ teaspoon sugar
- Salt to taste

## Instructions

### Prepare the Peppers
1. Preheat oven to 350°F (175°C).
2. Cut the tops off the peppers and carefully remove seeds and membranes.
3. If peppers don't stand upright, carefully trim a thin slice from the bottom (be careful not to create a hole).
4. Lightly brush peppers with olive oil and season inside with salt and pepper.
5. Arrange in a 9x13 inch baking dish.

### Make the Filling
1. In a large skillet over medium-high heat, brown the ground beef, breaking it into small pieces (5-6 minutes).
2. Add diced onion and cook until softened (3-4 minutes).
3. Add garlic and cook for another minute until fragrant.
4. Remove from heat and drain excess fat.
5. Stir in cooked rice, diced tomatoes, half of the tomato sauce, Worcestershire sauce, Italian seasoning, paprika, salt, and pepper.
6. Add 1 cup of mozzarella cheese and parsley. Mix well.

### Assemble and Bake
1. Spoon the filling generously into each pepper, mounding slightly on top.
2. Mix remaining tomato sauce with beef broth, tomato paste, and sugar.
3. Pour sauce around the peppers in the baking dish.
4. Cover tightly with aluminum foil.
5. Bake for 35 minutes.
6. Remove foil, top each pepper with remaining cheese.
7. Bake uncovered for 10 more minutes until cheese is melted and bubbly.
8. Let rest for 5 minutes before serving.

## Tips for Success

### Pepper Selection
- Choose peppers that are similar in size for even cooking
- Look for peppers with flat bottoms that stand upright
- Green peppers work best, but you can mix in red or yellow for color

### Make-Ahead Tips
- Prepare filling up to 2 days in advance
- Assemble stuffed peppers and refrigerate up to 24 hours before baking
- Add 10-15 minutes to baking time if starting from cold

### Rice Tips
- Use day-old rice for best texture
- Long-grain white rice works best
- For healthier option, substitute with brown rice or cauliflower rice
- Rice should be slightly undercooked as it will continue cooking in the oven

## Variations

### Protein Alternatives
- **Turkey:** Use ground turkey for a leaner option
- **Italian Sausage:** Replace half the beef with Italian sausage for extra flavor
- **Vegetarian:** Use plant-based ground meat or extra vegetables

### Grain Substitutions
- **Quinoa:** Replace rice with cooked quinoa for added protein
- **Orzo:** Use cooked orzo pasta for a different texture
- **Cauliflower Rice:** For low-carb option

### Cheese Options
- **Cheddar:** Sharp cheddar adds more flavor
- **Pepper Jack:** For a spicy kick
- **Parmesan:** Mix with mozzarella for depth

## Storage Instructions

### Refrigeration
- Store covered in refrigerator for up to 4 days
- Reheat in 350°F oven for 15-20 minutes until heated through
- Microwave individual portions for 2-3 minutes

### Freezing
- Wrap individual peppers in plastic wrap, then aluminum foil
- Freeze for up to 3 months
- Thaw overnight in refrigerator before reheating
- Bake from frozen at 350°F for 45-60 minutes (covered)

## Serving Suggestions
- Serve with crusty garlic bread
- Pair with a simple green salad
- Garnish with fresh basil or parsley
- Drizzle with extra sauce from the baking dish
- Serve alongside roasted vegetables

## Chef's Notes
"The key to perfect stuffed peppers is not overcooking them. They should be tender but still hold their shape. The foil covering during initial baking helps steam the peppers while keeping the filling moist. For a beautiful presentation, save the pepper tops and place them back on before the final cheese topping!"
