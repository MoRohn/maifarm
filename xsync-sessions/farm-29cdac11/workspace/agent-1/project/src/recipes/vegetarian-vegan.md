## 🌱 Quinoa Stuffed Green Peppers (Vegan)

### Overview
**Prep Time:** 25 minutes  
**Cook Time:** 40 minutes  
**Servings:** 6  
**Difficulty:** Easy  
**Diet:** Vegan, Gluten-Free

Protein-packed quinoa and vegetables make these stuffed peppers both nutritious and satisfying.

### Ingredients

#### For the Peppers
- 6 large green bell peppers, tops removed, seeds cleaned
- 2 tablespoons olive oil
- Salt and pepper to taste

#### For the Filling
- 1½ cups quinoa, cooked (about ½ cup dry)
- 1 can (15 oz) black beans, drained and rinsed
- 1 cup corn kernels (fresh or frozen)
- 1 can (14.5 oz) fire-roasted diced tomatoes
- 1 medium red onion, diced
- 3 cloves garlic, minced
- 1 cup mushrooms, chopped
- 2 teaspoons ground cumin
- 1 teaspoon smoked paprika
- 1 teaspoon chili powder
- ½ cup fresh cilantro, chopped
- 2 tablespoons nutritional yeast (optional)
- Juice of 1 lime
- Salt and pepper to taste

#### For Topping
- ½ cup breadcrumbs (use gluten-free if needed)
- 2 tablespoons olive oil
- ¼ cup pumpkin seeds
- 2 tablespoons nutritional yeast

### Instructions

1. **Prepare the peppers:**
   - Preheat oven to 375°F (190°C).
   - Brush peppers with oil, season with salt and pepper.
   - Place in a baking dish with ½ inch water.

2. **Make the filling:**
   - Heat 2 tablespoons oil in a large pan over medium heat.
   - Sauté onion until translucent (5 minutes).
   - Add mushrooms and garlic, cook for 3 minutes.
   - Add spices, cook for 1 minute until fragrant.
   - Stir in quinoa, black beans, corn, and tomatoes.
   - Cook for 5 minutes, stirring occasionally.
   - Remove from heat, add cilantro, lime juice, and nutritional yeast.
   - Season with salt and pepper.

3. **Assemble and bake:**
   - Fill each pepper with quinoa mixture.
   - Mix topping ingredients and sprinkle over peppers.
   - Cover with foil and bake for 30 minutes.
   - Remove foil and bake for 10 more minutes until topping is golden.

### Nutritional Information (per serving)
- Calories: 285
- Protein: 11g
- Carbohydrates: 48g
- Fat: 8g
- Fiber: 9g
- Iron: 4mg

## 🌮 Vegan Green Pepper Tacos

### Overview
**Prep Time:** 15 minutes  
**Cook Time:** 20 minutes  
**Servings:** 4 (8 tacos)  
**Difficulty:** Easy  
**Diet:** Vegan

Crispy roasted green peppers with spiced beans create delicious plant-based tacos.

### Ingredients

#### For the Peppers
- 3 large green bell peppers, sliced into strips
- 2 poblano peppers, sliced (optional for heat)
- 2 tablespoons olive oil
- 1 teaspoon ground cumin
- 1 teaspoon smoked paprika
- ½ teaspoon garlic powder
- Salt and pepper to taste

#### For the Bean Filling
- 1 can (15 oz) pinto beans, drained
- ½ cup vegetable broth
- 1 teaspoon chili powder
- ½ teaspoon oregano
- 2 tablespoons tomato paste
- Salt to taste

#### For Assembly
- 8 corn tortillas
- 1 avocado, sliced
- ½ cup red cabbage, shredded
- ¼ cup red onion, thinly sliced
- Fresh cilantro
- Lime wedges
- Hot sauce (optional)

#### Cashew Crema (Optional)
- ½ cup raw cashews, soaked 2 hours
- ¼ cup water
- 2 tablespoons lime juice
- 1 clove garlic
- Salt to taste

### Instructions

1. **Roast the peppers:**
   - Preheat oven to 425°F (220°C).
   - Toss pepper strips with oil and spices.
   - Spread on baking sheet and roast for 20 minutes.

2. **Prepare beans:**
   - Heat beans, broth, and spices in a small pot.
   - Simmer for 10 minutes, mashing partially.
   - Stir in tomato paste.

3. **Make cashew crema:**
   - Blend all crema ingredients until smooth.
   - Add water if needed for desired consistency.

4. **Assemble tacos:**
   - Warm tortillas on a griddle.
   - Spread beans on tortillas.
   - Top with roasted peppers, cabbage, onion, and avocado.
   - Drizzle with cashew crema.
   - Garnish with cilantro and serve with lime.

## 🥗 Mediterranean Green Pepper Salad (Vegetarian)

### Overview
**Prep Time:** 20 minutes  
**Cook Time:** 15 minutes  
**Servings:** 6  
**Difficulty:** Easy  
**Diet:** Vegetarian, Gluten-Free Option

A refreshing salad featuring roasted green peppers with Mediterranean flavors.

### Ingredients

#### For the Peppers
- 4 large green bell peppers
- 2 tablespoons olive oil

#### For the Salad
- 1 cup cherry tomatoes, halved
- 1 cucumber, diced
- ½ red onion, thinly sliced
- 1 cup chickpeas, cooked
- ½ cup Kalamata olives, pitted
- ½ cup feta cheese, crumbled (omit for vegan)
- ¼ cup fresh mint, chopped
- ¼ cup fresh parsley, chopped
- 2 tablespoons capers

#### For the Dressing
- ¼ cup extra virgin olive oil
- 3 tablespoons lemon juice
- 2 cloves garlic, minced
- 1 teaspoon Dijon mustard
- 1 teaspoon dried oregano
- Salt and black pepper to taste

### Instructions

1. **Roast the peppers:**
   - Char peppers over open flame or under broiler until skin is blackened.
   - Place in a bowl, cover with plastic wrap for 10 minutes.
   - Peel off skin, remove seeds, and cut into strips.

2. **Prepare salad:**
   - Combine roasted pepper strips with tomatoes, cucumber, onion, chickpeas, and olives.
   - Add herbs and capers.

3. **Make dressing:**
   - Whisk together all dressing ingredients.
   - Pour over salad and toss.
   - Top with feta cheese if using.
   - Let marinate for 30 minutes before serving.

### Serving Suggestions
- Serve with warm pita bread
- Add to grain bowls
- Use as a side for grilled vegetables

## 🍛 Green Pepper and Lentil Curry (Vegan)

### Overview
**Prep Time:** 15 minutes  
**Cook Time:** 35 minutes  
**Servings:** 4  
**Difficulty:** Easy  
**Diet:** Vegan, Gluten-Free

A hearty, protein-rich curry combining green peppers with red lentils in aromatic spices.

### Ingredients

- 3 large green bell peppers, cut into chunks
- 1 cup red lentils, rinsed
- 1 can (14 oz) coconut milk
- 1 cup vegetable broth
- 1 can (14.5 oz) diced tomatoes
- 1 large onion, diced
- 3 cloves garlic, minced
- 1 tablespoon ginger, minced
- 2 tablespoons curry powder
- 1 teaspoon ground turmeric
- 1 teaspoon ground coriander
- ½ teaspoon cayenne pepper (optional)
- 2 tablespoons coconut oil
- 1 cup spinach (optional)
- Salt to taste
- Fresh cilantro for garnish
- Juice of 1 lemon

### Instructions

1. Heat coconut oil in a large pot over medium heat.
2. Sauté onion until golden (5-6 minutes).
3. Add garlic and ginger, cook for 1 minute.
4. Add all spices, stir for 30 seconds until fragrant.
5. Add green peppers, cook for 5 minutes.
6. Add lentils, coconut milk, broth, and tomatoes.
7. Bring to a boil, then simmer for 25 minutes until lentils are tender.
8. Add spinach if using, cook until wilted.
9. Season with salt and lemon juice.
10. Garnish with cilantro and serve with rice or naan.

## 🥘 Ratatouille with Green Peppers (Vegan)

### Overview
**Prep Time:** 20 minutes  
**Cook Time:** 45 minutes  
**Servings:** 6  
**Difficulty:** Medium  
**Diet:** Vegan, Gluten-Free

Classic French vegetable stew highlighting green peppers alongside other summer vegetables.

### Ingredients

- 2 large green bell peppers, cut into chunks
- 1 medium eggplant, cubed
- 2 medium zucchini, sliced
- 1 yellow squash, sliced
- 3 large tomatoes, chopped
- 1 large onion, sliced
- 4 cloves garlic, minced
- ¼ cup olive oil
- 2 tablespoons tomato paste
- 1 teaspoon herbs de Provence
- 2 bay leaves
- Fresh thyme sprigs
- Fresh basil leaves
- Salt and black pepper
- ½ cup dry white wine (optional)

### Instructions

1. Heat olive oil in a large Dutch oven over medium heat.
2. Sauté onion until softened (5 minutes).
3. Add garlic, cook for 1 minute.
4. Add eggplant and peppers, cook for 10 minutes.
5. Add zucchini and yellow squash, cook for 5 minutes.
6. Stir in tomato paste and herbs.
7. Add tomatoes, wine (if using), and bay leaves.
8. Reduce heat to low, cover partially.
9. Simmer for 30 minutes, stirring occasionally.
10. Season with salt and pepper.
11. Garnish with fresh basil before serving.

### Serving Ideas
- Serve over polenta or pasta
- Use as a side dish
- Serve with crusty bread
- Top with vegan cheese

## Tips for Vegetarian & Vegan Green Pepper Cooking

### Enhancing Flavor
- **Roasting:** Brings out natural sweetness
- **Marinating:** Peppers absorb flavors well
- **Spices:** Don't be afraid to use bold spices
- **Umami:** Add nutritional yeast, miso, or soy sauce for depth

### Protein Additions
- Beans and legumes
- Quinoa and whole grains
- Nuts and seeds
- Tofu or tempeh

### Meal Prep Tips
- Roast peppers in bulk and freeze
- Pre-make fillings for quick assembly
- Most dishes reheat well for lunches

### Nutritional Benefits
- High in Vitamin C
- Good source of Vitamin A
- Contains antioxidants
- Low in calories, high in fiber
