Privacy Policy

Last Updated: January 7, 2026

Our Commitment: MaiFarm is designed with privacy as a core principle. Your API keys never leave your device, and we collect only the minimum data necessary to provide our service.

1. Introduction

Welcome to MaiFarm ("we," "our," or "us"). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application and related services (collectively, the "Service").

By using MaiFarm, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Information You Provide

  • Account Information: When you sign in with Apple, we receive your name (if you choose to share it) and email address (or Apple's private relay email).
  • Task Data: Descriptions of tasks you create, farm configurations, and harvested outputs.
  • Preferences: Your app settings and AI engine preferences.

2.2 Information Collected Automatically

  • Device Information: Device type, operating system version, and unique device identifiers for analytics.
  • Usage Data: How you interact with the app, features used, and session duration.
  • Crash Data: Technical information about app crashes to help us improve stability.

2.3 Information We Do NOT Collect

API Keys: Your AI provider API keys (Claude, OpenAI, etc.) are stored locally on your device in the secure iOS Keychain. We never transmit, store, or have access to your API keys.

3. How We Use Your Information

Data Type Purpose
Account Information To create and manage your account, authenticate you
Task Data To execute tasks and provide AI orchestration services
Device Information To optimize performance for your device
Usage Data To improve app features and user experience
Crash Data To fix bugs and improve app stability

4. Data Storage and Security

4.1 Local Storage

  • API keys are stored in the iOS Keychain with hardware encryption
  • Task history and preferences are stored locally on your device
  • Data can be synced via iCloud if you enable this feature

4.2 Server Storage

  • Account information is stored on secure servers
  • All data transmission uses TLS 1.2+ encryption
  • We use industry-standard security measures to protect your data

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information to third parties. We may share data in the following limited circumstances:

  • Service Providers: With vendors who help us operate our service (hosting, analytics), under strict confidentiality agreements
  • Legal Requirements: When required by law or to protect our rights
  • Business Transfers: In connection with a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets

6. Your Rights and Choices

6.1 Access and Portability

You can view your data within the app at any time. Contact us to request a copy of your data in a portable format.

6.2 Correction

You can update your account information in the app's Settings.

6.3 Deletion

You can delete your account and all associated data through Settings > Account > Delete Account. This action is permanent and cannot be undone.

6.4 Opt-Out

  • Disable analytics in Settings > Privacy
  • Disable iCloud sync in Settings > Sync
  • Disable notifications in iOS Settings

7. Children's Privacy

MaiFarm is not intended for children under 13 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe we have collected information from a child under 13, please contact us immediately.

8. International Data Transfers

Your information may be transferred to and processed in countries other than your own. We ensure appropriate safeguards are in place to protect your data in accordance with this Privacy Policy.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page and updating the "Last Updated" date. Continued use of the Service after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

  • Right to access your personal data

  • Right to erasure ("right to be forgotten")
  • Right to restrict processing

To exercise these rights, contact us at privacy@maifarm.app.

11. California Privacy Rights (CCPA)

California residents have specific rights regarding their personal information:

  • Right to know what personal information is collected
